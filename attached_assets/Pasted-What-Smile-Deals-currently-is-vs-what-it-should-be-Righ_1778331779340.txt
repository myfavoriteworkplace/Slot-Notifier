What Smile Deals currently is vs what it should be:
Right now the page is built as a patient-facing deals gallery — dark theme, cinematic hero card, tilt card animations, countdown timers, filter pills by procedure type. That design philosophy is correct for a B2C "save money on your cleaning" page. But you're now repositioning this as a B2B marketplace for dental suppliers — equipment vendors, supply companies, software providers, training organisations selling to clinic owners. These are fundamentally different audiences with different decision-making processes, different content needs, and different visual expectations.
A clinic owner evaluating a dental autoclave purchase does not want a cinematic tilt card with a countdown timer. They want specifications, supplier credibility signals, contact methods, and the ability to compare options. The current design is optimised for impulse booking. A B2B marketplace needs to be optimised for considered evaluation.
What the current page is missing entirely:
There is no supplier identity on the deal cards. Right now a deal shows a title, price, and image — but who is selling it? A clinic owner needs to know if this is a reputable supplier before they even read the description. Supplier name, logo, verified badge, and contact method are absent.
There is no product categorisation appropriate for B2B dental supplies. "Cleaning, Whitening, Braces, Implants" are patient procedure categories — completely wrong for a B2B audience. The correct categories are: Equipment (chairs, autoclaves, x-ray units), Consumables & Supplies, Orthodontic Products, Imaging & Radiology, Practice Management Software, Lab Services, Training & CPD, Sterilisation.
There is no lead generation or enquiry mechanism. B2B dental deals are not booked in seconds like patient appointments. A clinic owner interested in a dental chair needs to enquire, get a quote, possibly get a demo. The current "book a slot" CTA is the wrong action for B2B. The correct CTAs are: Request a Quote, Book a Demo, Download Brochure, WhatsApp Supplier, Call Now.
There is no featured/sponsored placement system that makes commercial sense for B2B. A supplier paying for premium placement needs a proper "Featured" or "Sponsored" badge, top-of-page visibility, and ideally a hero banner — not just a card with a slightly different border.
The empty state ("0 Active Deals") is currently showing on the live page. This is a conversion killer. Even before deals are populated, the page needs to communicate what kind of deals will appear here and invite suppliers to list.
What a proper B2B dental marketplace page needs — by section:
Section 1 — Hero/Header. Not the current patient-facing "Smile DEALS" headline. The B2B version needs: a headline addressing clinic owners directly ("Source dental supplies, equipment, and services — all in one place"), a subtitle about verified suppliers, and two CTAs: "Browse deals" (scrolls down) and "List your product" (opens supplier registration or contact). A search bar at this level for suppliers and product types.
Section 2 — Category navigation. Horizontal pill strip or icon grid with the 8 B2B categories listed above. Clicking filters the deals below. This is the primary navigation for a marketplace — users land and immediately orient themselves by category.
Section 3 — Featured/Sponsored deals. A dedicated strip at the top of the deals section for paid featured placements. Two or three cards maximum, clearly labelled "Featured" or "Sponsored" with a distinct visual treatment — slightly larger card, branded border, supplier logo prominent. This is your monetisation surface.
Section 4 — Deal grid. The main marketplace grid. Each card needs: supplier logo + name + verified badge, product/service name, short description (2 lines), pricing (or "Get quote" if price on request), category tag, CTA button (Request Quote / Book Demo / Contact Supplier), and a save/bookmark action. Three columns on desktop, two on tablet, one on mobile.
Section 5 — Supplier credibility strip. A "Verified suppliers" section showing logos of suppliers listed on the platform — even as a placeholder for now. This signals marketplace legitimacy to both buyers and potential new suppliers.
Section 6 — List your product CTA. A dedicated section inviting dental suppliers and equipment companies to list on the marketplace. "Are you a dental supplier? Reach 50+ verified clinics on bookMySlot." With a simple "Get listed" button. This is how the marketplace grows.
On the "For Clinics" / "For Patients" tab system currently on the page:
This is the right structural idea but the execution is wrong. Having both on the same page with tabs creates a split-focus problem — neither audience gets a page that feels built for them. My recommendation: make /smile-deals the B2B marketplace page entirely, and create a separate patient-facing deals section either on the booking page or as a distinct URL. The two audiences have such different needs that trying to serve both on one page dilutes both.
If you want to keep them on one page, the tabs should be much more clearly separated — more like two distinct page modes, not just a filter, with the tab switch changing the entire page layout, not just filtering cards.
Visual direction for the B2B marketplace:
Not the current dark cinematic theme — that's B2C. The correct aesthetic is clean, light, professional — matching your existing bookMySlot green/white design language from Image 3. Think of it like a product on IndiaMART or Amazon Business but with bookMySlot's design quality. White cards with clean borders, supplier logos prominent, green brand accents on CTAs and featured badges, a light grey background grid, and plenty of white space so individual deals breathe and can be scanned quickly.
The monetisation architecture I'd recommend:
Three listing tiers that map to your existing subscription thinking. Free listing — deal appears in the standard grid, standard card, supplier pays nothing. Featured listing — deal pinned to the top of its category, larger card format, supplier logo more prominent, "Featured" badge. ₹499–999/month. Sponsored placement — hero banner slot at the top of the page, maximum 1–2 per page at any time, full-width or half-width banner with rich media. ₹2,999–4,999/month. This gives suppliers a clear upgrade path and gives you a revenue stream beyond clinic subscriptions.
Summary of what I'd build:
A complete B2B dental marketplace page with: a supplier-addressed hero with search, category navigation strip (8 B2B categories), featured deals strip (monetisable), a main deal grid with proper supplier identity cards and B2B CTAs, a verified supplier logos strip, and a "Get listed" section for supplier acquisition. Light theme matching bookMySlot's existing design. Mobile responsive. Empty state that communicates the marketplace vision rather than just showing "0 Active Deals."
The patient-facing deals content moves to a separate section or page — keeping both audiences properly served without compromising either.
