Current Problems
Clinic Dashboard Card
The card is trying to show:
* Patient identity
* Appointment status
* Appointment schedule
* Contact details
* Doctor assignment
* Actions
All in the same visual weight.
Result:
* Eye doesn't know where to look first
* Important information gets buried
* Card height grows quickly

Doctor Dashboard Card
The card is cleaner but has opposite issues:
* Too much vertical whitespace
* Important patient context missing
* Complaint information is buried
* Missing operational actions
Doctors typically care about:
1. Patient
2. Appointment time
3. Chief complaint
4. Status
5. Records
not clinic metadata.
