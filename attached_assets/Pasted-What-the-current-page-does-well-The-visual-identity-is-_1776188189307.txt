What the current page does well: The visual identity is strong — the green brand colour, the bookMySlot DENTAL lockup, and the Doctor Portal UI mockup all feel cohesive. The stat callouts (850+ slots, 12 sec booking) are powerful social proof. The 3-step onboarding section is clean and scannable.
The core problem you've identified is right: The page reads as a slot booking tool, not a dental practice management platform. Specifically:
The hero headline "Booking made effortless" and the subtext are 100% about patient-facing booking. A clinic owner or practice manager landing here doesn't immediately see themselves — they see a product built for patients. The features section has practice management features buried in it (role-based access, data exports, doctor profiles) but they're presented identically to patient-facing features with no hierarchy. There's no second audience addressed — the page speaks to one person when it should speak to two: the clinic admin and the patient.
What I'd change and why, before building:
The headline needs a dual-audience pivot. Something like "Run your dental practice. Let patients book themselves." — the first half speaks to the clinic owner, the second half delivers the automation promise. This immediately signals that this is practice management software that includes patient booking, not just a booking widget.
A second section should be added specifically showcasing the practice management side — appointment history, patient records, doctor availability controls, reporting — with its own UI mockup. Right now the Doctor Portal mockup only shows the booking schedule, not the management depth.
The features grid should be split into two visual groups: "For your clinic" and "For your patients." Same features, reorganised so clinic owners immediately see their ROI.
The "Smile Deals" nav item is unique to your product and deserves a callout section — it's a differentiator that's currently invisible on the page.
The trust bar at top says "trusted by dental clinics" but has no number. Adding a specific clinic count (even "50+ clinics") converts this from decoration to social proof.
Everything else — colour, typography, layout structure, the 3-step section, the footer — I'd preserve exactly. The bones are good.
