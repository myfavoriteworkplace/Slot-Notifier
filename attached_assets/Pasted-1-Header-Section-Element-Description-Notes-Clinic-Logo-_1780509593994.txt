🧩 8. Visual & UX Guidelines
Color Palette: White background, soft green/blue accents.
Typography: Sans‑serif (Inter, Poppins, Roboto).
Layout: Card‑style blocks with subtle shadows and divider lines.
Icons: Minimalist icons for date, payment, prescription, and totals.
Responsive Design: Auto‑fit for A4 PDF and mobile view.
Accessibility: High contrast for text, readable font sizes.
