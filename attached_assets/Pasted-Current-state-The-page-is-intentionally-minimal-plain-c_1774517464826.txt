Current state
The page is intentionally minimal — plain centered text, two stock shadcn Cards, no background treatment. Compared to everywhere else in the app it has:
* No hero banner
* No gradient or neon accent bars
* No glow blobs
* Cards with no colour, no gradient header, no depth
* A generic font-bold heading with no visual hierarchy

What the rest of the app consistently does
Every major page (Book, ClinicLogin, ClinicDashboard, Admin Smile Deals) follows the same pattern:
1. 3px neon bar at the top — from-accent via-primary to-accent
2. Dark gradient hero block — from-primary/90 via-primary to-accent/80 with a subtle radial white glow overlay and a decorative large icon faded in the corner
3. Eyebrow label — small caps, wide tracking, text-white/60
4. Bold headline + subdued subline — all white against the hero background
5. Bottom gradient divider closing the hero
6. Cards with a coloured gradient header strip, icon avatar with glow, and border-primary/25 shadow-primary/8 depth
