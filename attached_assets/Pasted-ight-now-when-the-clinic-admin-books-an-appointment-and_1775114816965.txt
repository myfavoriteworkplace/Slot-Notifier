How it will work
When the clinic admin creates an appointment and assigns it to a doctor, that appointment will not go directly into the doctor's confirmed list anymore. Instead it will sit in a waiting area and the doctor will get a notification saying there is a new appointment waiting for their approval.
The doctor will see a new section on their dashboard called Awaiting Approval. They can look at the appointment details and either accept it or decline it. If they accept it, it moves into their regular appointments list and the patient gets notified that their appointment is confirmed. If they decline it, the clinic admin gets notified and can reassign it to another doctor.

The admin still has full control
The clinic admin does not have to wait for the doctor. If the admin wants to confirm the appointment themselves on behalf of the doctor, they can do that directly from the admin panel just like they do today. The only difference is that when the admin confirms it, the doctor gets a heads up notification saying the admin has confirmed an appointment on their behalf so they are not surprised when they see it in their schedule.

What the doctor will see differently
On the doctor's dashboard there will be a new amber coloured card in the stats row showing how many appointments are waiting for their approval. There will also be a new Awaiting tab in the filter section. The appointments waiting for approval will only appear there with Accept and Decline buttons. The regular Appointments section will only show appointments that are already confirmed so it stays clean and uncluttered.

What happens to existing appointments
Nothing changes for any appointment that already exists in the system. They will all continue to show as confirmed exactly as they do today. This new flow only applies to new appointments created from this point forward.

In one sentence
New appointments wait for either the doctor or the admin to confirm them before they appear in the doctor's schedule, and whoever confirms it the other person gets notified so everyone is always on the same page.
