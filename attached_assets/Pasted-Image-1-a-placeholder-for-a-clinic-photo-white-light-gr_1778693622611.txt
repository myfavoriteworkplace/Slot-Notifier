The delivery format:
Rather than individual image files (which are heavy and don't scale), I'll build this as a single self-contained HTML file that renders all assets as inline SVGs. You open it in a browser, see every asset rendered at multiple sizes, and can copy the SVG code for each one directly into your templates. This is the professional way — SVGs are 200–300 bytes each, render perfectly at any size, and need no external dependencies.
What makes these futuristic and standard simultaneously:
I'll use the geometric precision aesthetic — thin strokes, rounded terminals, consistent 1.5px stroke weight, 24px base grid, subtle radii on enclosing shapes. This is the Lucide/Heroicons standard that Linear, Vercel, and modern SaaS products use. Not flat emoji, not thick cartoon icons — clean technical line art with your brand's green accent. The placeholder cards will have a low-opacity gradient wash and a subtle animated shimmer (CSS only, no JS) so they feel alive rather than broken.
