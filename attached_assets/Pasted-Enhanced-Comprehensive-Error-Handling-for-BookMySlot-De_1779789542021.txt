Additional Recommended Scenarios

Location Permission Denied
→ "Location access is needed to show nearby clinics. You can still search manually."
Doctor on Leave
→ "Dr. X is on leave. Here are other available doctors on this date."
Appointment Cancellation Limit Reached
→ "You have reached the maximum cancellations for this month."
Prescription Upload (Follow-up)
→ "Please upload your previous prescription for better consultation."
Age Validation (Senior Citizen Discount)
→ "Senior citizen discount applied for patients above 60 years."


Design & UX Principles (Notification Layer)

Tone: Empathetic, calm, and solution-oriented (never robotic).
Visuals: Use soft icons (warning, info, error) + consistent color coding.
Red/Orange → Critical
Yellow → Warning
Cyan/Blue → Info

Structure: Icon + Short Title + Helpful Message + Action Button(s)
Persistence: Important errors (payment failed, session expired) should not auto-dismiss.
Offline Mode: Allow browsing past bookings even when offline.
